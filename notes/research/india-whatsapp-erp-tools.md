# India: WhatsApp-to-ERP tools, Indic AI building blocks and platform rules (construction / interiors / small contractors)

Research date: 21 October 2026. All claims are dated where the source gives a date. "Vendor claim" = the company's own website, launch page or blog. "Independent" = press, regulator, third-party analysis or open benchmark. Third-party BSP / competitor blogs are marked as such because they have a stake in the answer.

## 1. Indian construction / interior PM and ERP apps: features, WhatsApp, AI, pricing, funding, users. Do any read WhatsApp groups automatically?

### Takeaway
The Indian construction/interior PM apps (Powerplay, Onsite, RDash) all position themselves as a *replacement* for WhatsApp-plus-Excel site coordination, not as a layer that reads WhatsApp groups. None of the sources found shows any of them automatically reading WhatsApp group chats; their WhatsApp touchpoints are outbound sharing, support or unspecified "integrations", and their AI features are either absent or vague. That leaves the "read the existing WhatsApp group and update the ERP" job unclaimed by the incumbents found.

## 2. WhatsApp-based AI bookkeeping / bill capture for Indian SMBs; accuracy on handwritten "kacha" bills, GST, approval step

### Takeaway
There are small WhatsApp-first bill-capture tools (Zoffer Bills -> Zoho Books, Blipko, AI Accountant), but the big SMB bookkeeping apps (Vyapar, myBillBook) do bill OCR *inside their own app*, and Khatabook/OkCredit/Tally use WhatsApp only to *send* invoices and reminders. The pattern that has emerged is "AI reads bill -> human taps approve -> entry posts", and no public, independent benchmark of handwritten Indian bill accuracy was found; all accuracy numbers are vendor claims.

### Cited Findings
- **Zoffer Bills** (Product Hunt launch, 2026): forward a vendor bill to Zoffer on WhatsApp; AI reads printed or handwritten bills, extracts vendor, date, line items, tax and amount, and creates the entry in Zoho Books. Nothing is posted until the user taps approve on a summary. ₹999/month. Claims 1,000+ bills processed in beta. Founder story: a hired data-entry person entered two months of bills under the wrong GST codes, discovered only at filing. — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/products/zoffer-bills) (vendor claims)
- **Blipko**: described as an "AI-powered digital accountant designed specifically for Indian SMBs" that runs as a WhatsApp chatbot; listed as an MIT-licensed open-source project with activity through Dec 2025; no funding found. — [Peerlist](https://peerlist.io/sadikkp/project/blipko); [goodfirstissue listing](https://www.goodfirstissue.org/square-story/blipko)
- **BeyondAccount**: Product Hunt listing "run your entire accounting on WhatsApp without accountant". — [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/p/beyondaccount-accounting-on-whatsapp/beyondaccount-accounting-on-whatsapp) (not examined further)
- **AI Accountant** (works with Tally and Zoho): claims 93-99% field accuracy on handwritten invoices with GST validations. — [AI Accountant blog](https://www.aiaccountant.com/blog/handwritten-invoice-processing-india) (vendor claim, no test set published)
- **Vyapar**: in-app OCR; upload a PDF or image, it fills vendor, item and GST details for review; claims 99.9% accuracy. Not a WhatsApp channel. — [Vyapar OCR page](https://vyaparapp.in/ocr-scanner-software) (vendor claim)
- **myBillBook**: June 2025 changelog added purchase-bill OCR on Android (photo or PDF), with review/edit before saving. — [myBillBook changelog, 25 Jun 2025](https://knowledge.mybillbook.in/changelog/product-updates-jun-25-2025)
- **Khatabook / OkCredit**: WhatsApp used to share invoices and send payment reminders; no WhatsApp bill-scan feature found. — [OkCredit llms.txt](https://okcredit.in/llms.txt); [Khatabook Play listing via Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/app/google/com.vaibhavkalpe.android.khatabook/kuwait/)
- **TallyPrime**: from Release 4.0, send invoices/reports to parties on WhatsApp via pre-approved templates; requires a WABA through BSP Interakt; outbound only (no attachments added manually, no scheduling); paid plans listed from ~USD 36-54/year by region. — [Tally help](https://help.tallysolutions.com/tally-prime/tallyprime-with-whatsapp/share-documents-using-whatsapp-for-business/); [Tally product page](https://tallysolutions.com/global/tallyprime-with-whatsapp-business/)
- **Zoho Expense**: receipt autoscan via phone photo, email forwarding, Chrome/Zoho Mail add-ons; Standard plan "currently available in India only", up to 20 scans/user/month. No WhatsApp capture found. — [Zoho blog](https://blog.zoho.com/expense/introducing-receipt-autoscan-for-free-and-standard-plans.html)
- **Fyle** (Bengaluru-founded): launched a WhatsApp expense integration in 2019; current help docs describe text-message receipt submission for US and Canadian orgs only. — [CPA Practice Advisor, 9 Oct 2019](https://www.cpapracticeadvisor.com/?p=35314); [Fyle help](https://help.fylehq.com/en/articles/8045065-submit-your-receipts-via-text-message)
- **Accuracy evidence (non-independent)**: a consultancy blog reports, per invoice (one wrong field = invoice wrong), handwritten notes/corrections at 62% for classic OCR, 87% for LLM, 85% hybrid; no dataset details. — [RaftLabs](https://www.raftlabs.com/blog/ocr-vs-llm-invoice-processing)
- LLM extraction risks noted: hallucinated fields and numeric errors; recommendation to check totals against tax arithmetic and validate GSTINs. — [AI Accountant](https://www.aiaccountant.com/blog/ai-based-invoice-data-extraction) (vendor guidance)
- A public "kirana receipt" fine-tune (dukaan-saathi-receipt-lora) was trained on 6 hand-written + 22 synthetic examples, i.e. no meaningful accuracy evidence. — [Featherless model page](https://featherless.ai/models/Zappandy/dukaan-saathi-receipt-lora)

### Inferences
- The approval step is now table stakes: Zoffer, Vyapar and myBillBook all put a human review before posting. For a construction group bot, "extract -> confirm in chat -> post" matches the market norm.
- Deterministic checks (line items sum to total, GST arithmetic, GSTIN checksum/format, vendor matched to master) are the realistic guard against LLM numeric errors on kacha bills, since no trustworthy accuracy figure exists.
- Integration targets that Indian SMBs already use are Tally and Zoho Books; Onsite also charges ₹20,000/yr for exactly these integrations (see section 1).

### Gaps
- No independent benchmark of handwritten Indian bill extraction (Hindi/Kannada/English mixed, GST and non-GST) was found. An honest answer would be "we would measure it on our own labelled set".
- Khatabook / OkCredit / Vyapar 2026 AI roadmaps (if any WhatsApp ingestion exists) were not found on public pages.
- Zoffer Bills' company details (founders, funding, location) not found beyond the launch page.

## 3. WhatsApp group tooling used in India (Periskope, Wati, Interakt, AiSensy, Gallabox, DoubleTick): group support, official vs unofficial access

### Takeaway
There are two routes. (1) Official: Meta's Cloud API Groups API (2026) limits groups to **8 participants** and requires an Official Business Account; DoubleTick sells this. (2) Unofficial: tools like Periskope connect ordinary WhatsApp / WhatsApp Business app numbers (not WABA) and can sit in large existing groups, which is what a "read the project group" bot actually needs, but automating the consumer app is against WhatsApp's terms and carries ban risk. The big Indian BSPs (Wati, Interakt, AiSensy) are built around 1:1 template/inbox messaging on the official API.

### Cited Findings
**Official Groups API (Meta)**
- Eligibility: businesses with an Official Business Account (OBA); not available for WhatsApp Business app numbers or numbers onboarded to Multi-solution Conversations. Max 8 participants per group, max 10,000 groups per business number, max one Cloud API business per group. Supports send/receive, pin, mark-read; text, media and template messages; not interactive messages, calling, disappearing or view-once. Groups use per-message pricing (separate rate page). — [Meta Groups API docs](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/groups/)
- Groups are invite-only; participants join via an invite link the business sends. — [Meta Groups API docs, via search](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/groups.md)
- Third-party analysis (2026): "available, but mostly blocked by OBA" (written against earlier May 2026 wording). — [Kapso blog](https://kapso.ai/blog/whatsapp-groups-api-state-2026)
- A third party says the business number itself takes one of the 8 slots (not confirmed in Meta's page). — [Unipile](https://www.unipile.com/whatsapp-group-api/)

**DoubleTick (Indian BSP)**
- Groups on the official Cloud API number; "not regular mobile WhatsApp groups"; requires Cloud API number not in coexistence mode, and OBA. PRO and ENTERPRISE plans include 5 groups; extra groups ₹50/group/month. — [DoubleTick changelog](https://roadmap.doubletick.io/changelog); [DoubleTick learn](https://learn.doubletick.io/whatsapp-groups/understanding-whatsapp-groups)
- Group webhook events (e.g. GROUP_PARTICIPANT_JOINED) available only for Cloud API integrations, not via Gupshup or 360dialog. — [DoubleTick docs](https://docs.doubletick.io/reference/whatsapp-group-webhooks)

**Periskope (WhatsApp groups tool; CEO Swapnika Nag)**
- Built for WhatsApp group and community management; works with normal WhatsApp numbers (regular and Business app), explicitly does not support WABA numbers; API access needs a phone connected via QR code. API can create groups, add/remove members, send messages, raise tickets. — [Periskope API docs](https://docs.periskope.app/api-reference/introduction); [Periskope groups page](https://periskope.app/whatsapp-groups)
- Features: central inbox for groups, tickets created from group chats with AI flagging, AI first-level agent (uses Google Gemini), data export, integrations with HubSpot, Freshdesk, Zoho, Slack, Google Sheets, Zapier; "no cost per message". Vendor claims 5,000+ businesses in 50+ countries; claims GDPR, ISO 27001 and "DPDP compliant". No ban-risk discussion on the page. — [Periskope groups page](https://periskope.app/whatsapp-groups)
- Funding: $1.2M total, pre-seed, last round Jan 2023 (single aggregator). — [VCBacked](https://www.vcbacked.co/company/periskope)
- The Ken podcast featured Periskope on "WhatsApp's double life" (business use of ordinary WhatsApp). — [The Ken](https://the-ken.com/podcasts/two-by-two/whatsapps-double-life/)

**Unofficial access risk**
- whatsapp-web.js and Baileys automate the consumer app, which breaks WhatsApp's terms; their own READMEs state no guarantee against blocking; detection is heuristic (strangers messaged, block/report rate) rather than a fixed cap. — [developer guide](https://whatsapp.checkleaked.cc/ja/blog/avoid-whatsapp-ban) (third party)
- Official Cloud API via Meta or a BSP is the only sanctioned automation route. — [Omnichat](https://blog.omnichat.ai/unofficial-whatsapp-business-api/) (BSP blog, interested party)

**Wati / Interakt / AiSensy (official-API BSPs) pricing (third-party, conflicting, 2026)**
- Interakt starter ~₹2,499/mo (one source) or $55/mo Growth (another); AiSensy Pro ~₹3,200-3,499/mo + GST with ~30% hike in May 2026 and ₹750/mo per agent beyond five; Wati Growth $49-59/mo, Pro ~₹5,999/mo. Meta message fees billed on top. — [RichAutomate comparison, Jun 2026](https://richautomate.in/compare/wati-vs-aisensy-vs-interakt-vs-richautomate); [go4whatsup, Sep 2026](https://www.go4whatsup.com/india/whatsapp-business-api-guide-2026/); [CodingClave on Wati](https://codingclave.com/blog/wati-pricing-india-2026) (all competitor/third-party blogs)
- Interakt is owned by Jio Haptik; TallyPrime uses Interakt as its WhatsApp BSP. — [Levitation directory](https://levitation.in/top-conversational-ai-voice-bot-companies-india); [Tally help](https://help.tallysolutions.com/tally-prime/tallyprime-with-whatsapp/share-documents-using-whatsapp-for-business/)

### Inferences
- A typical interior project group (client, designer, PM, site supervisor, carpenter, electrician, vendors) easily exceeds 8 people, so the official Groups API cannot host a real project group today. Realistic official designs are: (a) a 1:1 bot that people forward messages/voice notes/bills to, or (b) a small official "core team" group. Reading existing large groups means an unofficial linked-device connection (Periskope-style), with ToS and ban risk on the number used.
- The interview answer should name this trade-off explicitly; it is the main platform constraint for the product.

### Gaps
- Gallabox group support was not researched; Wati, Interakt and AiSensy group features were not confirmed either way on their own pages.
- Periskope's pricing and exact connection mechanism (QR/linked device implied by its API docs) not stated on its marketing page.
- Whether Meta delivers *all* group messages to the business webhook is not stated on the Groups API page.

## 4. WhatsApp Business Platform in India: pricing since July 2025, INR costs, Groups API, user counts

### Takeaway
Since 1 July 2025 Meta charges per delivered template message; from 1 October 2026 it also charges utility messages inside the 24-hour window and service messages beyond 1,000 free per number per month. India's marketing rate is about ₹0.86 and utility/authentication about ₹0.115 per message (before 18% GST and BSP markup), and India accounts must move to INR billing by 31 December 2026. A bot that mostly *receives* messages and replies within the window is cheap; the October 2026 change makes high-volume replies no longer free.

### Cited Findings
- Per-message pricing by category (marketing, utility, authentication, service, Meta Business Agent) and recipient country; user-initiated messages are not charged; undelivered messages not charged. — [Meta pricing docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing) (fetched 21 Oct 2026)
- 24-hour customer service window resets with each user message; free-entry-point window of up to 7 days after Click-to-WhatsApp ads. Each business number gets 1,000 free service messages/month (no rollover). — [Meta pricing docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing)
- **1 Oct 2026 changes**: utility messages inside an open service window become charged (free until 30 Sep 2026); service messages beyond the 1,000 monthly free tier become charged; numbers without a payment method by 30 Sep 2026 may have service delivery stopped. Governments and non-profits keep free service messages through 31 Dec 2027. — [Meta pricing docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing); also reported by [ChatMaxima](https://chatmaxima.com/whatsapp-api-pricing/india/)
- Meta Business Agent messages charged at $2.00 per million tokens (~4-5 US cents per message). — [Meta pricing docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing)
- India dates: marketing rate increased 1 Jan 2026; INR billing launched 1 Jan 2026; all India messaging accounts must migrate to INR by 31 Dec 2026, after which Meta stops delivering messages from non-INR accounts. Rates change only on 1 Jan / 1 Apr / 1 Jul / 1 Oct. — [Meta pricing docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing)
- India rates (third-party, excluding 18% GST): marketing ₹0.8631 (up ~10% from ₹0.7846 on 1 Jan 2026); utility ~₹0.115; authentication ~₹0.115; service free in window (pre-Oct 2026). Marketing with GST ≈ ₹1.02. BSPs typically add 10-30% markup. — [Monty Mobile](https://montymobile.com/blogs/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-in-india-inr-rates-gst-and-the-2026-currency-migration-deadline); [Whautomate](https://whautomate.com/whatsapp-business-api-pricing-india) (third-party; Meta's page does not print the India numbers, they are in its rate-card file)
- Groups API: see section 3 (OBA only, 8 participants, per-message pricing). — [Meta Groups API docs](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/groups/)
- India user count: the only WhatsApp-confirmed India figure found is "over 400 million" (older TechCrunch report); 2026 third-party estimates range 535M-854M and conflict. Global: 3 billion+ monthly users announced by Meta in May 2025. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=1860803); [Hyperleap stats blog](https://hyperleap.ai/blog/whatsapp-statistics-india-2026); [Infobip](https://infobip.com/blog/whatsapp-statistics)

### Inferences
- Unit economics for a contractor-facing bot: inbound messages free; one confirmation reply per extracted item would, from Oct 2026, cost a utility/service rate of roughly ₹0.1 per message beyond free tiers, i.e. small next to LLM/STT cost per voice note (see section 5).
- The INR migration deadline (31 Dec 2026) is a practical setup item for any new India WABA.

### Gaps
- The exact India service-message rate after 1 Oct 2026 and the Groups API per-message rate were not visible on the fetched page (they sit in Meta's rate-card CSV/PDF).
- No current official Meta figure for WhatsApp users in India.

## 5. Indic AI building blocks: Sarvam, AI4Bharat, Bhashini, Krutrim; Google/OpenAI on Hinglish speech; handwritten bill OCR

### Takeaway
Sarvam offers a priced, India-hosted stack that matches the bot's needs: speech-to-text with a code-mix mode at ₹30/hour, document extraction at ₹0.5-1 per page, and its own 105B LLM at ₹15/₹60 per million input/output tokens. Open models (AI4Bharat IndicConformer, MIT, 22 languages) allow self-hosting. On conversational Hinglish, published WERs cluster around 11-14% for the best systems (Sarvam Saaras v3 ~11.3%, ElevenLabs Scribe v2 ~12.4%) versus ~30% for Whisper large-v3, but these are vendor-published numbers and none was found that directly scores GPT-4o-transcribe or Gemini on code-mixed audio.

### Cited Findings
**Sarvam AI (official pricing docs, fetched 21 Oct 2026; all INR)**
- Speech-to-text ₹30/hour; with diarization ₹45/hour; speech-to-text + translate ₹30/hour; + diarization ₹45/hour. Billed per second. — [Sarvam pricing docs](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api-reference-docs/pricing)
- LLMs per 1M tokens (input / cached / output): Sarvam 105B ₹15 / ₹5 / ₹60; also hosts Gemma 4 31B, GLM-5.3, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (beta). — [Sarvam pricing docs](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api-reference-docs/pricing)
- Text-to-speech Bulbul v3 ₹30 per 10K characters; translation (Sarvam Translate / Mayura) ₹20 per 10K characters; language ID ₹3.5 per 10K. — [Sarvam pricing docs](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api-reference-docs/pricing)
- Document Digitization API ₹0.5/page, Document Extraction API ₹1/page (max 10 pages/job). Sarvam's marketing pages instead show Sarvam Vision at ₹1.50/page — conflict. — [Sarvam pricing docs](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api-reference-docs/pricing); [Sarvam API pricing page](https://sarvam.ai/api-pricing)
- Free credits: docs say ₹100 for new users; marketing page said ₹1,000 — conflict. — [Sarvam pricing docs](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api-reference-docs/pricing); [Sarvam API pricing page](https://sarvam.ai/api-pricing)

## 6. DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025: implications for a bot that reads group chats

### Takeaway
The DPDP Rules were notified on 13 November 2025 with staggered commencement: consent-manager provisions on 13 November 2026 and the main obligations (notice, consent, purpose limitation, security safeguards, breach reporting, data-principal rights, retention/erasure, penalties) on 13 May 2027. A government written answer in August 2026 said these dates will not move; MeitY's January 2026 proposal to compress 18 months to 12 had not been notified. For a group-reading bot this means every person whose messages, voice and phone number are processed (site workers, vendors, clients) is a data principal who needs notice and a lawful basis, and the bot operator needs processor contracts, retention limits and breach procedures in place before May 2027.

### Cited Findings
- DPDP Rules 2025 notified 13 Nov 2025 (G.S.R. 846(E)); Rules 1, 2 and 17-21 (definitions, Data Protection Board) in force on publication. — [Conventus Law overview](https://conventuslaw.com/featured-content/building-indias-digital-privacy-framework-an-overview-of-the-dpdp-rules-2025/); [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india%2Fcentre-notifies-dpdp-rules-implementation-planned-in-phases-spread-over-12-18-months-3798465)

### Inferences
- Group chats contain third parties' personal data (names, phone numbers, voice, photos with faces, addresses of client homes). The firm running the project (firstbriq) would be the data fiduciary; the bot vendor/LLM/STT providers are processors needing contracts.
- Practical design implications: post a clear notice in each group when the bot is added (with a link in Indian languages), extract only project-relevant fields, keep raw audio/images only as long as needed, log provenance of each ERP entry, and allow deletion on request. Building this before the 13 May 2027 deadline is cheaper than retrofitting.
- Using an unofficial linked-device connection (section 3) to read groups where not everyone has been notified is the weakest point both for WhatsApp ToS and DPDP notice/consent.
